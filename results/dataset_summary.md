Own-clip frames in train/val: **0** (must be 0; `own` only writes `test_own`). Detector and finger splits are pooled per split, so a source image used for training either model counts as training data.

HaGRID images keep HaGRID's subject-disjoint split (by `user_id`, per the dataset authors), so no person appears in more than one split.

## Sources and licenses

- **HaGRIDv2** (512 px version): hand boxes and gestures. HaGRIDv2, variant of CC BY-SA 4.0 (see github.com/hukenovs/hagrid/tree/master/license). Kapitanov et al., *HaGRID: HAnd Gesture Recognition Image Dataset*, WACV 2024; Nuzhdin et al., *HaGRIDv2: 1M Images for Static and Dynamic Hand Gesture Recognition*, arXiv:2412.01508. Splits follow HaGRID's subject-disjoint train/val/test.
- **COCO 2017** `sports ball`: annotations CC BY 4.0, images under their Flickr licenses. Images whose largest person covers > 15% of the frame are dropped because COCO does not label hands.
- **Own clips** (`test_own`): recorded with `scripts/record_clips.py`; the only held-out test data that matches the drone use case.
